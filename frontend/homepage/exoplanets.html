55 Cancri e

"The Diamond Planet"

Twice the size of Earth and 8 times as massive, this super-Earth is believed to be made largely of carbon. Due to unimaginable internal pressure and temperatures exceeding 2,400°C, a significant portion of the planet has likely crystallized into pure, solid diamond.

Gliese 436 b

"The Burning Ice"

A Neptune-sized exoplanet shrouded in a paradox. It orbits so close to its star that its surface temperature is around 439°C (822°F). Yet, its immense gravity compresses its water so tightly that it forms a solid state known as "Ice-X". It is literally made of burning, solid ice.

TrES-2b

"The Pitch Black Planet"

The darkest known exoplanet in the universe. It reflects less than 1% of the light that hits it, making it significantly darker than coal or the blackest acrylic paint. The only light it emits is a faint, ominous deep red glow due to its blistering 1,000°C atmosphere.
